# Photograph credits

## Bundled welcome photograph

The bundled welcome_photo.webp is adapted from scikit-image's coffee.png,
photographed by Rachel Michetti, courtesy of Pikolo Espresso Bar.
It is available under CC0.

- Source: https://github.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/blob/v0.16.2/skimage/data/coffee.png
- Attribution and licence: https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.coffee

The image was converted to WebP for the on-device welcome screen.

## Store screenshot photograph

The store screenshots use **Temples and Towers at Sunrise**, photographed by
**NPS Photo / Rebecca Alfafara**, from Zion National Park.
The National Park Service identifies the photograph as public domain with full
reuse rights.

- Source: [NPS photograph page](https://www.nps.gov/media/photo/gallery-item.htm?gid=98B2CD6B-155D-451F-67A73851F18C9948&id=9863d408-155d-451f-67df-89cc4a0175b1&pg=0)
- Original: [3648 x 2736 JPEG](https://www.nps.gov/npgallery/GetAsset/9863d408-155d-451f-67df-89cc4a0175b1)
- Rights metadata: [NPS gallery metadata](https://www.nps.gov/npgallery/api/search/execute/albumid/98B2CD6B-155D-451F-67A73851F18C9948?pagesize=500&primarytype=image), asset `9863d408-155d-451f-67df-89cc4a0175b1`; `ConstraintsInformation` specifies `Public domain` and `Full` granting rights.

The original was centre-cropped to 2189 x 2736 pixels for a 4:5 portrait
composition, without upscaling. The screenshots show the photograph inside
FilmLab, including film looks and edits applied by the app.
